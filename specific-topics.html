The standing utility benchmark — Q-CTRL × IBM 3,000× Fermi-Hubbard speedup on 120 qubits — remains in force, and Google's Willow Quantum Echoes (13,000× over classical, in Nature) continues to be cited as the algorithmic-circuit champion.

A separate experimental result this week demonstrated a stable quantum-encryption system across 120 km of optical fiber, a meaningful step toward real-world QKD deployments.
