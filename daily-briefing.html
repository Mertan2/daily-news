Beijing summitPutin–Xi sign "multipolar world" declaration; Power of Siberia-2 route agreed, price not

Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping signed a 47-page joint declaration in Beijing Wednesday on building a "multipolar world and a new type of international relations" at the close of Putin's two-day state visit — the 25th anniversary of the Treaty of Good-Neighbourliness and Friendly Cooperation. The declaration criticised the US-Israeli strikes on Iran and endorsed Moscow's "root causes" framing on Ukraine. On energy: Russia is "ready to continue reliable, uninterrupted supplies" of oil, gas (incl. LNG), and coal to China. Critical caveat: the two sides failed to clinch the big Power of Siberia-2 gas deal Moscow had flagged for "detailed discussion." An understanding was reached on the route and construction of the pipeline (~50 bcm/yr Russian gas via Mongolia), but pricing details remain unresolved — a continuation, not a resolution, of the most-watched bilateral commercial pivot of 2026.
