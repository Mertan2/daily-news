Two things widened alongside it. How to read it: the "pause" was never a ceasefire, and this week showed why the distinction mattered. What survived the restart is the shipping channel — Iranian and Omani deputy foreign ministers are still talking about Hormuz traffic management, and 14 commercial vessels transited on Wednesday against single digits last week. De-escalation on shipping and escalation on strikes are running in parallel, not sequentially.
