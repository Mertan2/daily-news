Science · Space

NASA's Psyche flies by Mars today at 12,333 mph for a gravity assist

NASA's Psyche spacecraft makes its closest approach to Mars today — passing just 2,800 miles (4,500 km) above the surface at 12,333 mph. The flyby is a gravity assist that steals a fraction of Mars's orbital momentum to bend Psyche's trajectory toward its target asteroid 16 Psyche, which the spacecraft is scheduled to reach in 2029. Engineers will use today's pass to calibrate the multispectral imager and gamma-ray spectrometer against a known body for the first time on a real, large-scale target — capturing thousands of Mars images for instrument-tuning. NASA's "Eyes on the Solar System" portal is streaming the encounter. NASA Eyes · EarthSky · NASA Psyche blog
