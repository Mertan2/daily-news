EU orders Google to share Search data and open Android to rival AI assistants

The European Commission on July 16 issued two binding DMA specification decisions requiring Google to give third-party AI assistants the same Android access Gemini enjoys — including voice activation and in-app actions such as booking a taxi — and to share anonymised ranking, query, click and view data from Google Search with rival search engines and AI chatbots.

It is the most consequential DMA enforcement action yet, and a template for how Europe intends to regulate AI distribution.
