Running Threads

Iran — Trump "negotiating on fumes," MOU signing slips twice; ceasefire fraying on Lebanon track

Thursday update (May 28): At yesterday's televised Cabinet meeting, Trump said the US is "negotiating on fumes" with Iran and "will give it a few more days," insisting "the midterms will not impact my war strategy." The White House signing event for the 60-day MOU (phased Strait of Hormuz reopening, frozen-funds release, 30-60 day window for verifiable uranium-stockpile and enrichment-suspension talks) had been trailed for Sunday May 24 — slipped to mid-week, then slipped again. Iran's foreign ministry called fresh US overflights and Tuesday's Israeli airstrike package in southern Lebanon a "grave violation" of the April-16 ceasefire. Secretary of State Rubio: "a deal is still possible." Brent −5.55% to $88.68 Wednesday on the cooling tone — five-week low, off more than $20 from the early-May spike. Remaining sticking points: Tehran wants a permanent end to the war before nuclear negotiations; Washington wants the renunciation up front; Hormuz mine-clearing sequencing; frozen-assets release sequencing. Pentagon's strike options remain "ready" per the May 24 readout; some forces pulled from region as MOU hedge. Iraqi base staging hub still active.

Trump-Zelenskyy framework still ~90-95% aligned; final 5-10% on territorial recognition language and security-guarantee architecture remain unresolved.

Israel-Lebanon — IDF deepens ground operation past "yellow line"; cumulative deaths past 3,070

Tuesday May 26: Israel pounded Lebanon with 120+ airstrikes in one of the heaviest days in weeks — IDF says hit 100+ Hezbollah sites across the south and eastern Bekaa (storage, command, observation posts). ≥31 killed, 40 wounded per Lebanese Ministry of Public Health. Wednesday IDF expanded the ground operation past the so-called "yellow line"; advance displacement orders issued for Tyre and surrounding areas north of the Zahrani River (~40 km from the Israeli border). Hezbollah claimed 32 counter-operations Tuesday (rocket, artillery, drone). Cumulative Lebanese deaths since the March-2 resumption now past 3,070; over 1 million displaced. April-16 ceasefire frayed but not formally broken — Netanyahu: military operations "deepening." Iran FM Araghchi to Hezbollah's Naim Qassem: Tehran "will not give up its support."

Türkiye — CHP standoff Day 8; Bilgi closure decree rescinded but transfers proceed

Thursday update (May 28): Eight days after the May 21 Ankara court ruling voiding the 2023 CHP primary and reinstating Kılıçdaroğlu, ousted leader Özgür Özel rules out founding a new party, calling on members to "stay and fight." He demands the chair be elected by all 2M members and a congress called 15 days after Eid. Sunday riot police battered into CHP HQ in Ankara firing tear gas; Tuesday's İzmir rally was met with water cannon before Özel could address the crowd. Pravda Turkey reports procedural moves could see Özel stripped of parliamentary immunity and his seat. TIME headline: "Türkiye's deepening democratic crisis." More than a dozen CHP mayors arrested since the party's 2024 local sweep. Bilgi University: Erdoğan's May 22 closure decree was rescinded by a second Official Gazette decree May 25 — but operations and student transfers to Mimar Sinan Güzel Sanatlar Üniversitesi as guarantor proceed. Underlying Can Holding organised-crime/money-laundering probe (and the arrest of ex-rector Mehmet Remzi Sanver) continues. YÖK transfer mechanics still unsettled; thousands of students in administrative limbo.

Quantum & neuro — Harvard pulls fault-tolerance timeline forward; first two-way adaptive BCI
