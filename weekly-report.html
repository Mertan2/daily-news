Daily Log

Monday, June 29
  • Iran · truce breaksThe interim accord collapsed into open fire: after US strikes inside Iran on Saturday, Iran's Revolutionary Guard hit US bases with missiles and drones early Sunday — the Ali Al Salem airbase in Kuwait and the Fifth Fleet in Bahrain. Tehran threatened a "complete halt" to talks; Trump warned Iran "will no longer exist" if attacks continue. The 60-day roadmap (full-deal ~Aug 22) is now in open jeopardy; oil set up for a sharp repricing.
  • Global · AI washoutA global AI sell-off deepened: SoftBank cratered ~12.5% and the Nikkei sank 4.15% to 69,361 on reports OpenAI may delay its IPO to 2027; Seoul's KOSPI hit repeated circuit breakers as Samsung (−12%) and SK Hynix (−12.5%) slumped. US indexes had ended Friday roughly flat (S&P 7,354.02).
  • China · purge & crashThe NPC stripped six PLA generals of their seats (incl. space-program chief Xu Xueqiang) plus a Politburo member — an extending Xi purge. Days earlier a light plane slammed into Beijing's 528m CITIC Tower (pilot killed, 13 hurt, footage scrubbed), and a China–Russia bomber patrol scrambled Japanese and Korean jets.
  • Japan · quakeAn M6.1 quake struck off Iwate Sunday (max lower-5, no tsunami), days after the June 25 M7.2 Sanriku quake; PM Takaichi ordered damage checks as officials warned of week-long aftershocks. The yen sat near ¥161.7, close to 1986 lows, despite the BOJ at 1%.
  • SerbiaPresident Vučić pledged to resign within weeks and call early elections after ~18 months of anti-corruption protests — but thousands kept marching Sunday, calling it a "power grab in disguise" amid the largest protest wave since Milošević's fall.
  • EuropeA skydiving plane crash near Nancy killed all 11 aboard (France's deadliest in ~30 years); the record heat dome set a 41.3°C German national high with hundreds of deaths reported; Ukraine's "40-day operation" drove a record drone assault (Russia claimed 660 downed) and a Crimea state of emergency.
  • TürkiyeThe NATO Parliamentary speakers' summit opened in Istanbul (Erdoğan hosting at Dolmabahçe), but an Ankara court ordered 178 pre-summit detentions of activists, lawyers and a journalist, drawing HRW criticism. Trump hinted at an F-35 breakthrough; the lira held near 46.64 with the CBRT at 37%.
  • USThree federal firefighters were killed in a Colorado "burnover"; Julia Letlow won the Louisiana GOP Senate runoff (~56.9%); and the Supreme Court closed the term with 6–3 immigration wins (TPS, reentry), with birthright citizenship and the Lisa Cook firing still pending.
  • SportThe World Cup Round of 32 opened: Canada 1–0 South Africa (95th-min winner) — first knockout match; South Korea out in the group stage (President Lee apologized), Japan through. June 29 slate: Brazil–Japan, Germany–Paraguay, Netherlands–Morocco. India's women were knocked out of the T20 World Cup at Lord's.
Tuesday, June 30 today
  • Iran · de-escalationThe weekend exchange pulled back from the brink: after Saturday's US strikes and Iran's Sunday missile/drone barrage on US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, both sides agreed to talk. Trump said US and Iranian negotiators meet in Doha today, with envoy Witkoff en route and Hormuz traffic "consistent." Oil rose on relief (Brent +1.3% to ~$72.91). The sticking point is now the Lebanon linkage — Tehran wants a full Israeli withdrawal before signing.
  • US · landmark rulingThe Supreme Court overturned the 91-year-old Humphrey's Executor precedent (6–3), letting the president fire independent-agency heads at will — a major concentration of executive power. But in a 5–4 carve-out it spared Fed Governor Lisa Cook, who stays pending litigation. It also upheld Mississippi's mail-ballot grace period (Trump pushed the "SAVE America Act"). The term's final day is today, with birthright citizenship still pending.
  • Markets · reboundWall Street roared back Monday: the S&P rose 1.15% to 7,439, the Nasdaq +2%, and the Dow closed above 52,000 for the first time as Alphabet debuted in the index (+5%). Investors bought the AI dip (Tesla +8.5%, Amazon +3.2%) on Gulf de-escalation. Tokyo lagged — the Nikkei fell a 2nd day to 69,175 before the US bounce.
  • Japan · quakeA strong M6.9 quake (max upper-6) struck off Iwate around 7:30 a.m., shaking Aomori hardest — no tsunami. It intensifies the sequence after the June 25 M7.2 Sanriku quake; PM Takaichi ordered damage checks as JMA warns of week-long aftershocks.
  • Europe · heatThe record heat dome's toll mounted: WHO said 1,300+ excess deaths across Europe since June 21 (France ~1,000, Spain 327; 85% aged 65+), after France hit 44.3°C and Germany 41.3°C.
  • China · PMI beatJune factory activity firmed: manufacturing PMI 50.3 (vs 50.0), high-tech 53.5 on AI-hardware demand — even as the PLA purge widened (six generals stripped of NPC seats; ~100 officers touched since 2022).
  • Europe · Ukraine & SerbiaRussian strikes killed at least 8 in Ukraine (5 in Dnipro, 3 in Zaporizhzhia); Saab signed a $4.83bn submarine deal with Poland. In Serbia, protests pressed on in Kraljevo despite Vučić's resignation pledge, dismissed as "a power grab in disguise."
  • TürkiyeOne week from the NATO summit (Ankara, July 7–8), HRW reported 209 detained and Ankara banned all gatherings through July 10. The lira topped 46/USD with the CBRT on hold at 37%.
  • SportWorld Cup Round of 32: Brazil 2–1 Japan (Martinelli stoppage winner, Japan out); Germany out on penalties to Paraguay; Morocco beat the Netherlands on penalties. June 30 slate: France–Sweden, USA–Bosnia, Mexico–Ecuador, England–DR Congo and more.
