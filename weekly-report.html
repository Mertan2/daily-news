Weekly Report

Week of May 11 – 17, 2026

A running log of the week's major stories — updated each day. Last revision: Friday, May 15.

Running Threads

Trump-Xi Beijing summit — closes Friday with separate readouts, 200 Boeing jets, no joint communiqué

Trump and Xi held a closing private tour of Zhongnanhai Friday before Air Force One lifted off Beijing Capital — ending a two-day state visit that delivered separate readouts rather than a joint communiqué. Trump told Fox News that Xi committed to 200 Boeing jets (Wall Street had floated 500; Boeing closed –3.8%), "double-digit billion" annual US-ag purchases over three years, additional US energy/oil imports, and a private guarantee not to supply Iran with military equipment. Xi accepted Trump's invitation for a Washington state visit on September 24, 2026. Beijing's readout led with Taiwan as "the most important issue" — explicit Thucydides-trap framing about "clashes and even conflicts"; the White House readout omitted Taiwan entirely. Mutual Hormuz "open and non-militarised" pledge holds. Nvidia H200 export thaw structured with 25% of revenue routing to the US Treasury on ~10 Chinese-firm sales (Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance, JD.com); Beijing-side guidance has so far halted deliveries. Markets read the trip as stabilising, not breakthrough.

Iran war & Hormuz — first crack opens

The Chinese VLCC Yuan Hua Hu sailed out of the strait past Iran's Larak Island into the Gulf of Oman on May 13 — the rare exit timed to Trump-Xi summit prep, consistent with Iran's March 26 carve-out for China/Russia/India/Iraq/Pakistan-flagged vessels. But it's one tanker, not a reopening. Strait operating well below normal capacity May 11-13; Gen. Caine confirmed 22,500 mariners on >1,550 vessels still stranded. ADNOC's Sultan Al Jaber: Iran imposing >$1M/ship tolls, ~230 tankers waiting inside the Gulf. Trump's May 6 pause of "Project Freedom" still holds. U.S. cost-of-war tracker hit $29B (Pentagon comptroller). EIA still warning crude could rise another $20/bbl if the chokepoint persists. Pump prices ~$1.50/gal above pre-war baseline.

Russia–Ukraine — biggest two-day aerial onslaught of the war; Kyiv high-rise collapse

The May 9–11 US-brokered ceasefire expired late on the 11th. Overnight May 11–12, Russia fired 216 drones; the May 13–14 assault escalated to ~56 missiles + 675 Shahed drones; the combined two-day onslaught into Friday morning totals 1,560+ drones plus 56 missiles — the single largest two-day aerial assault of the war. Friday-morning emergency-service revisions: 24 dead (including three children), 47 wounded in Kyiv, with an entire stairwell collapsed and 18 apartments destroyed in the Darnytskyi district. Two injured in Odesa region; port infrastructure at Chornomorsk hit. Ukrainian air-defence intercepted 94% of drones, 73% of missiles. Ukraine struck back at Russian gas-industry sites in Orenburg, 1,500 km inside Russia, earlier in the week. FM Sybiha still pushing a European-brokered mutual airport-strike pause; the Kremlin reiterated Donbas-withdrawal precondition for any new ceasefire. Trump administration silent on the strikes through Friday. Cumulative Russian troop losses since Feb 24 2022: 1,345,240 (1,060 in the last 24h).

Sarmat ICBM test — Putin disclosure, late-2026 deployment

The RS-28 Sarmat (Satan II) ICBM test from May 12 was disclosed late Wednesday. Putin claims the missile's MIRVed payload is "more than four times" any Western counterpart and an operational range exceeding 35,000 km. Combat deployment targeted for late 2026 against an extant Voyevoda fleet of ~40 launchers. The disclosure timing coincides with New START expiry and the Beijing summit.

UK political crisis — Streeting walks; Burnham return engineered, Rayner cleared

By May 12, 88 Labour MPs and four junior ministers had called on PM Starmer to step down after the May local-election losses (~1,500 council seats, mostly to Reform UK). Streeting met Starmer privately May 12, then publicly backed off a challenge. On May 14, Streeting formally resigned as Health Secretary, asserting Starmer "will not lead the Labour Party into the next general election." On May 15, Labour MP Josh Simons announced he will resign his Commons seat to free a return path for Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham; separately Angela Rayner confirmed HMRC has cleared her of any wrongdoing in the tax inquiry that forced her cabinet exit. Resignation count from the May crisis: five (4 junior + Streeting). Survation overnight: Starmer beats Streeting head-to-head among members 53–23 — the field of potential challengers is wider than the winner-list. Eurasia Group keeps 80% Starmer-ouster probability. Gilt yields climbing. UK Q1 GDP printed +0.6% q/q Thursday (best in over a year, beat 0.4% forecast), briefly steadying the economic argument.

S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 printed all-time highs Monday May 11. April CPI on the 12th: +0.6% m/m / +3.8% y/y — highest annual since May 2023; core +0.4%/+2.8%. Walmart Connect ads +31%. Q2 sales guide +3.5–4.5%; declined to provide EPS guide on tariff volatility. Cisco's 4,000-cut wave began; FY26 AI-orders guide raised to ~$9B from $5B.

Neurotech — Paradromics into humans

On May 14, Paradromics completed its first in-human implant of the Connexus BCI at the University of Michigan — a 420-electrode device placed in the temporal lobe of an epilepsy patient already in surgery, ~20 min implant+remove time, recording at single-neuron resolution. Surgeon: Dr. Oren Sagher. Research lead: Dr. Matthew Willsey. Connexus reports >200 bps (≈20× Neuralink's initial demo). Neuralink itself disclosed in April: 45 enrolled across three continents; 85% of spinal-injury participants hitting pick-and-place within 150% of non-injured baselines; next-gen surgical robot capable of reaching virtually any brain region. Cambridge hafnium-oxide neuromorphic chip claim from May 13: 70% AI-energy cut.

Quantum — Q-CTRL/IBM remains the "useful advantage" anchor; Kyoto's W-state breakthrough

Q-CTRL × IBM: 3,000× speedup on 120-qubit Fermi-Hubbard materials simulation — still the standing "useful quantum advantage" benchmark. JUPITER (Germany) classically simulates a 50-qubit machine. Google Willow Quantum Echoes 13,000× over classical, in *Nature*. New May 13: Kyoto University + Hiroshima University built a tabletop photonic circuit that performs one-shot identification of 3-photon W states by exploiting cyclic shift symmetry through a photonic quantum-Fourier-transform circuit. The method generalises to any number of photons — unblocks a long-standing bottleneck for scalable quantum networks. Harvard separately: field advancing faster than expected. IBM's May 4 "Decade of Quantum on the Cloud" anchors the broader trajectory.

Hantavirus cluster — case count flat at 11/3

MV Hondius cluster (WHO DON600): 11 cases (9 confirmed + 2 probable + 1 inconclusive), 3 deaths (2 lab-confirmed Andes virus). Pathogen confirmed as Andes hantavirus — only hantavirus known to spread human-to-human, possibly airborne in close-contact settings. ECDC May 13 update confirms passenger disembarkation + repatriation completed by May 11 after Tenerife arrival; CDC issued HAN00528 Level 3 classifying response, US public-health risk rated "extremely low." Repatriation hit 6 European countries + Canada. No new clusters and no community spread anywhere as of May 14. Tedros: "not another Covid." Surveillance window still open as repatriated passengers complete incubation.

EU sanctions & Russia — 20th package live

EU's 20th Russia-sanctions package takes effect May 14: transaction bans on 20 additional Russian banks plus institutions in Laos, Kyrgyzstan and Azerbaijan; expanded restrictions on non-financial firms facilitating cross-border payments. The package is aimed at the shadow-fleet workarounds that re-emerged since the 19th round.

Türkiye — CBRT lifts 2026 inflation; Erdoğan in Astana, OTS summit in Turkistan Friday

CBRT Governor Karahan presented the Q2 Inflation Report May 14 in Istanbul, lifting 2026 year-end CPI forecast to 26% (from 18%), 2027 to 15%, 2028 to 9%, citing Iran-war fallout. April CPI was 32.37% y/y; CBRT held policy rate at 37%. USD/TRY hit fresh record 45.42 on the 13th; BIST holds near record ~15,040. Erdoğan in Astana May 13–14 co-chairing the 6th Türkiye-Kazakhstan Strategic Cooperation Council with Tokayev — 13 agreements signed, bilateral trade up nearly 9% to >€4.5B; Türkiye and Kazakhstan also signed a joint drone-production agreement via SAHA. Erdoğan received the inaugural Khoja Ahmed Yasawi Order. On Friday May 15, OTS informal summit opens in Turkistan under theme "AI & Digital Development." Akçakale-Tel Abyad Syria gate reopened May 13 after 12 years closed. PKK May 12 announced disbandment-and-disarmament; İmamoğlu's X account still blocked inside Türkiye. Galatasaray clinched a third-straight Süper Lig title; final round Sunday.

Nikkei 225 +0.84% to ~63,272 — third straight record close; Olympus +~20% on buyback/upgrade, Kioxia +9.5%. JGB yields: 10-yr 2.55% (29-yr high), 30-yr 3.436% (record), 40-yr 3.689% (record).
