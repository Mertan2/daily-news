Weekly Report

Week of June 29 – July 5, 2026

A running log of the week's major stories — updated each day. Latest revision: Saturday, July 4. Where last week left off (June 22–28): the June US–Iran war had cooled into a fragile paper truce — the "Islamabad memorandum" lifted the naval blockade, reopened the Strait of Hormuz and set a 60-day nuclear-talks window — but it frayed all week, climaxing Friday when US aircraft struck Iranian sites after Iranian drones hit Hormuz shipping, even as Israel, Lebanon and the US signed a trilateral framework for a partial IDF pullback. Central banks turned hawkish in unison (BOJ to 1.0%, a Warsh Fed leaning toward hikes, the ECB to 2.25%); a hot May PCE (core ~3.4%) collided with a global AI-stock washout (Seoul circuit breakers, Tokyo's record 72,366 surrendered) even after Micron's blowout. Europe's worst-ever heatwave broke national records; UK Labour's leadership crisis deepened after Starmer's resignation; Türkiye's CHP nullity fight moved to the courts; a M7.2/M5.6 quake run shook Japan; and DRC Ebola passed 1,150 cases. This week opens with the truce breaking into a direct US–Iran exchange.
